# ContextWeaver adoption evidence

This file records **genuine external use**, not popularity signals.

Current verified public adopters: **0**.

That is an evidence state, not a product verdict. Per #855, absence of adoption is
not interpreted as weak demand until qualified distribution has been tested well
enough to distinguish no reach from product/value failure.

## Qualification

An entry may be added only when an independent project/team has gone beyond a
star, fork, download, synthetic demo, maintainer-created integration, or casual
positive comment.

Statuses:

- **evaluating** — testing D1 with a real or representative capability source;
- **integrated** — using snapshot/diff/inspect/verify in a genuine workflow;
- **retained** — independently active after approximately 30 days;
- **former** — evaluated/integrated and later removed or abandoned.

A narrower retained behavior should be named explicitly, for example
`diff-only diagnostic` rather than implying the full D1 workflow is retained.

## Evidence rules

- Public listing requires explicit permission or a claimant-owned submission.
- Former/removed evaluations remain useful evidence and should not be hidden.
- Do not expose proprietary capability names, schemas, prompts, customer data,
  credentials, or internal metrics.
- Stars, forks, downloads, clones, contributor activity, and AI-authored demos
  are not adoption evidence.
- High-touch design partners and unassisted evaluators must remain distinct in
  aggregate reporting.

See #551 for adoption/retention rules, #658 for first-success measurement, and
#855 for the distribution-quality gate.
